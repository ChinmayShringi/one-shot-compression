Sidecar split

experiments/paint_list_n3/sidecar/shared_edges.json is written by the generator
at fixture-write time (write_fixtures.py, which imports generator_n3.py).

The encoder path is encode.encode(px, width, height). It accepts only raster
bytes. encode.py does not import generator_n3, does not read the seed, and
does not open the sidecar.

The checker does not open experiments/paint_list_n3/sidecar. Shared-edge
count is computed from the committed pixel rasters: two distinct same-color
shapes whose painted pixels touch along a 4-connected edge. Corner-only
contact does not count. Hash match uses decode of the committed programs
against the committed fixture bytes.

Image 16 was regenerated under seed 20260910 as a rectangle and ellipse that
share a 4-edge. The existing fitter does not recover that raster. Frozen
constants were not retuned. The paint list stops at disconnected or
two-rectangle masks.
