Sidecar split

experiments/paint_list_n3/sidecar/shared_edges.json is written by the generator
at fixture-write time (write_fixtures.py, which imports generator_n3.py).

The encoder path is encode.encode(px, width, height). It accepts only raster
bytes. encode.py does not import generator_n3, does not read the seed, and
does not open the sidecar.

The checker reads the sidecar only to report how many held-out images have
two same-color shapes that share an edge. Hash match uses decode of the
committed programs against the committed fixture bytes.
